Mile Marathon

The 49th Annual Mile Marathon will be on May 19th, 2026

What is the Mile Marathon?

This amazing one-mile run for Columbine students started in 1977, and is even one year older than the Bolder Boulder! Rich Castro, one of the founders of the Mile Marathon, figured out the Mile Marathon is the longest running children’s foot race west of the Mississippi. It is fun for the children and at the end students get to sign each others shirts.

Fundraising

The Mile Marathon is also the PTA’s largest fundraiser of the year! Thank you to all of the families and friends of Columbine students who donate, and to our incredible 2025 sponsors.

For more information go to milemarathon.com.
