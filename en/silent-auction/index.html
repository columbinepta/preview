Silent Auction + Nachos Night

Silent Auction + Nachos Night Date: Thursday, February 27, 2025 Location: Columbine Elementary Cafeteria Admission: FREE for all families!

Join Us for a Fun Family Evening!

Come together for one of Columbine PTA’s amazing community events! Enjoy delicious food, browse amazing auction items, and connect with other Columbine families for a fantastic evening of fun and fundraising.

What to Expect

Silent Auction

  • Browse and bid on incredible items donated by local businesses and families
  • Gift baskets, gift certificates, experiences, and more!
  • All proceeds support Columbine Elementary programs and classroom grants
  • Door prizes throughout the evening - everyone has a chance to win!

Delicious Food

  • Nachos bar with all the fixings
  • Cotton candy station
  • Additional food options available

How to Participate

Before the Event:

  • Email us at columbineptaboulder@gmail.com if you’re interested in volunteering
  • Consider what you’d like to bid on and bring payment method

During the Event:

  • Place your bids throughout the evening
  • Enjoy dinner with your family
  • Stay until the end to see if you won!

Donations Needed: We’re seeking donations for our silent auction! If you own a business or can donate items, gift certificates, or services, please contact [contact info]. All donations are tax-deductible.
